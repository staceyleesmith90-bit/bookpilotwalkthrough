# Welcome to Reel Studio 🎬

Reel Studio turns Claude into your personal reel editor. You film; Claude cuts, captions,
styles and adds stickers and sound, in **your** brand. The first time you open it, Claude
interviews you (about 10 minutes) to set up your look. Here's what to have ready.

## Have these ready (5 minutes of prep)

**1. Your brand basics** (whatever you have; skip what you don't)
- ☐ Your **website link** (Claude can read your colours, fonts and logo from it), **or**
- ☐ Your **logo** as a file (PNG with a transparent background is best; JPG is fine)
- ☐ Your **brand colours** as hex codes if you know them (like `#1E63FF`). Don't know them?
  Claude pulls them from your website or logo.
- ☐ Your **fonts**, if you use specific ones. If they're Google Fonts, Claude fetches them.
  Not on Google (a bought or custom font)? Have the font file ready (.ttf or .otf) and upload
  it in the Studio under **My brand → Fonts**. Stickers use your brand fonts automatically, or
  you can pick the sticker font there.
- ☐ **No brand yet?** No problem. Think of 3 words for how you want your content to feel
  (e.g. *warm, honest, fun*). Claude suggests 3 complete looks for you to choose from.

**2. Your voice** (so captions and hooks sound like you)
- ☐ Who you talk to (e.g. "busy moms starting a side business")
- ☐ 3 words that describe how you talk, and any words you'd never use
- ☐ How you like to end videos (e.g. "Comment GUIDE for the free guide", "Follow for more")

**3. Inspiration (optional, but it helps a lot)**
- ☐ Links to 2–5 reels you love the style of. Claude studies their pacing, colours and
  text style, and edits yours in a similar way (with your brand, never copying their content).

**4. Your first video**
- ☐ One talking-to-camera clip (30 seconds to 3 minutes) filmed on your phone, vertical.
  Messy is fine: pauses, restarts and "um"s get cut automatically.

**5. Music (optional: free music is built in)**
- Nothing needed: describe the music ("cosy lo-fi, slow") and Reel Studio composes it free.
- ☐ Want a **trending TikTok sound** instead? Note the song name from TikTok. Reel Studio
  suggests the best one for each reel and gives you a no-music version to add it to in TikTok.
- ☐ Optional: your own music files (mp3) you have the rights to, or an ElevenLabs account for
  studio-grade AI music (paid per track). Claude helps you connect it.

## What you need on your computer
- A Mac (macOS 13+) or Windows 10/11 PC with ~5 GB free space
- A paid Claude plan (the Pro plan is a good start) with **Claude Code** (desktop app)
- The setup lesson installs the free tools this needs (Python, ffmpeg)

## How a reel works
0. Open the **Studio app** (Claude opens it for you, or run `python -m engine studio`),
   press **＋ New reel** and answer the quick questions: format, video, what it's about, the
   vibe, a title style, music and captions. Or skip the app and just talk to Claude:
1. Put your video in the **inbox** folder (or drop it into the chat) and say *"edit this reel"*.
2. Claude cuts it and opens a **review page**: remove lines, add notes, then **Send my edits**.
3. Claude shows a **style plan** in plain words. Say *"build it"*.
4. You get a finished reel + cover image. Want to move something? Say *"open the editor"*:
   drag your logo, stickers and text anywhere, change timing, and render.

**Good to know**
- **Every sticker exists.** If a sticker isn't in the library, Reel Studio creates your own:
  instantly as a lettered sticker in your brand font and colours, then Claude draws an
  illustrated one that stays in your library.
- **Trending sounds:** Studio → *Trending sounds* opens TikTok's official list. Save the ones you
  like; Claude suggests one per reel. Post the `final-for-trending-sound.mp4` file and add the
  sound in TikTok (business accounts: pick "approved for business" sounds).

You never need editing jargon. Talk to Claude like you'd text a friend:
*"make it calmer"*, *"bigger text on the money part"*, *"add my logo bottom right"*,
*"make it look like this reel [link]"*.
