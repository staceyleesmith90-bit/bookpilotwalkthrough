# Welcome to Reelmate 🎬

Your reel-editing buddy: you film it, Reelmate edits it, with Claude.

**Everything you need is on one page:** open `docs/Reelmate-Quick-Start.pdf`.

The short version:
1. Double-click the Reelmate file → Claude asks to install → **Install** → paste your key.
2. In Claude, say **"hi Reelmate"**. It sets itself up and asks a few easy questions.
3. Add your clips (or paste a Google Drive link), say what the reel is about, tick the storyboard, done.

Stuck? Just ask Claude: "how do I…?"
