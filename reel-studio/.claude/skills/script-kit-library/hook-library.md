# The Hook Library

Forty-five hook patterns, fifteen for each job. Each one names the pattern, the levers it pulls, what it does, a
fill-in line, Meg's own line where she has one, and two translations into other niches so you can see it travel.
Thirty-four more live in `more-hooks.md`, the bonus set.

## How to use it

1. **Pick the job first:** reach, grow or convert (see `method.md`). Most hooks come from that job's fifteen.
2. **Check "Use it for".** An opener starts the reel. A proof beat goes right after the hook. An ask goes after
   the payoff, never in the hook.
3. **Fill the brackets with your own moment, words and facts.** Meg's line shows the pattern working in her
   niche, and the translations show it moving to others. Neither is a line to use, even in the same niche.
4. **Stack two or three levers** where it comes naturally (see `levers.md`).
5. **Run the pre-ship check** in `method.md` before you film.

The translations use a working cast: a fitness coach, a candle maker, a realtor, and a faith and family
creator. They are examples, not real people. Any number that would be a claim stays in brackets. The candle maker's lines show a pattern traveling into her own story. On a real product seller's account, a story like hers is told for her customers, and its hook block names them, never other makers (see "Who the reel is for" in `method.md`).

# Reach

Built to be found by new people. Usually a short on-screen line over b-roll, 5 to 10 seconds. Judge it by plays
against your median for that format.

### R1 · The Exact Hour
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** An exact, slightly odd time drops the viewer into a moment they've lived. The time stamp stops
  the thumb before the thought even lands.
- **Fill in:** `[Your people] at [exact hour]: [the thought running on a loop].`
- **Meg's line:** "Moms at 2am: thinking... how do I re-start my career and make money without actually leaving
  my baby." (on-screen line, 1.6M+ plays, her most-watched reel)
- **Fitness coach:** "Moms at 5:40am: one workout before the house wakes up, or ten more minutes of sleep."
- **Realtor:** "Buyers at 11pm: scrolling the same three listings, doing the mortgage math again."

### R2 · The Roll Call
- **Levers:** identity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Calls a group by an oddly specific marker they share. The right people feel their name called
  across a crowded room.
- **Fill in:** `For the [people] who [an oddly specific thing they share]:`
- **Meg's line:** "Dear Algorithm, please introduce me to moms born between 1985-1999..." (on-screen line,
  900K+ plays)
- **Candle maker:** "For the people who won't light a candle because it's too pretty to burn:"
- **Faith and family creator:** "For the moms who pray in the car because it's the only quiet room:"

### R3 · The Unnamed Feeling
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Swaps the obvious word for a truer one. The viewer gets a label for something they've felt,
  and the relief keeps them watching.
- **Fill in:** `It wasn't [the obvious word]. It was [the truer label].`
- **Meg's line:** "They said it was the baby blues. It wasn't." (spoken, 170K+ plays)
- **Fitness coach:** "It's not a motivation problem. It's a Tuesday-at-6pm problem."
- **Realtor:** "It's not cold feet. It's leaving the house your kids grew up in."

### R4 · The Before and After Self
- **Levers:** identity, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names who they were and who they're becoming in one breath, with the thing that changed them
  squeezed in between. It's about identity, not tactics.
- **Fill in:** `[Old self]. Then [what happened]. Now [who they're becoming].`
- **Meg's line:** "I spent 10 years climbing a ladder. Maternity leave is when I finally looked up. And what I saw
  stopped me." (spoken, 32K+ plays)
- **Candle maker:** "I balanced spreadsheets for [number] years. Then I poured one candle for a friend's shower."
- **Faith and family creator:** "I used to say I'd never homeschool. Now school happens at our kitchen table."

### R5 · The She Diary
- **Levers:** self-relevance, identity · **Use it for:** opener, often on screen
- **What it does:** Tells it in the third person. "She" lets viewers recognize themselves without feeling pointed
  at.
- **Fill in:** `This is [the person]. She [what her days hold]. But [the quiet thing she misses or wants].`
- **Meg's line:** "This is the stay at home mom. She gets to be there for all of it." (spoken over a whiteboard,
  250K+ plays)
- **Fitness coach:** "She ran marathons before kids. Now she runs to the car in the rain and calls it cardio."
- **Realtor:** "She's rented for [number] years, and she still reads the listings every Sunday night."

### R6 · The Ordinary Scene
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A small, specific everyday moment with a big feeling underneath. People stop because they
  recognize it, not because they were baited.
- **Fill in:** `[Time or place]. [A tiny everyday moment]. [The feeling humming under it].`
- **Meg's line:** "The family dinners, the ordinary Tuesday, the kid's sick day." (spoken, 250K+ plays)
- **Candle maker:** "Kitchen table, 9pm. Wax cooling in twelve jars. And for once, the house is quiet."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Sunday, 8:52am. Shoes on three kids. Mine still in my hand."

### R7 · The Unspoken Truth
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Promises the part of the season people live but never say out loud. The hush is the hook.
- **Fill in:** `The part of [this season] nobody says out loud: [the true thing].`
- **Fitness coach:** "The part of postpartum nobody says out loud: I miss my old body, and I'm proud of this one."
- **Realtor:** "The part of buying a first home nobody says out loud: the first night feels like a hotel."

### R8 · The Search Bar
- **Levers:** self-relevance, social proof · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Shows what they'd really type into a search bar late at night. It reads like a private
  confession. The crowd version tells them they're not the only one searching.
- **Fill in:** `What I typed at [time]: "[the real, slightly embarrassing search]."` Or the crowd version:
  `Apparently a lot of [people] are searching for [this].`
- **Meg's line (the crowd version):** "Apparently a lot of moms are searching for this. I'm calling it the third
  option." (spoken, 54K+ plays, 1,200+ comments)
- **Candle maker:** "What I typed at midnight: 'why does my candle tunnel down the middle.'"
- **Faith and family creator:** "What I typed at 1am: 'how do you pray when you're too tired to think.'"

### R9 · The Notes App
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Reveals a private note they've kept and never said out loud. It feels like reading a diary
  over their shoulder.
- **Fill in:** `There's a note in my phone called "[title]." It says [the private thing], and I've never said it out loud.`
- **Fitness coach:** "There's a note in my phone called 'someday.' It says: run a half marathon."
- **Realtor:** "The note in my phone called 'our house' has one line. A porch swing."

### R10 · Us and Them
- **Levers:** identity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Draws the line between the people who get it and everyone else. Viewers stop to be claimed,
  then share it to say "this is my group." Keep it warm: claim your people, never mock the other side.
- **Fill in:** `[Your people] get this. [Everyone else] never will.`
- **Meg's line:** "We were raised for the boxes, not the middle." (spoken, 250K+ plays)
- **Candle maker:** "If you've ever bought a candle just for the name on the label, you're one of us."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Moms who've shushed a toddler through an entire sermon get this."

### R11 · The Denial Stack
- **Levers:** pattern interrupt, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Clears the wrong answers one short line at a time, so the real one lands harder. The rhythm
  alone stops the scroll.
- **Fill in:** `Not [wrong reason]. Not [wrong reason]. Not even [wrong reason]. [The real one].`
- **Meg's line:** "Not lack of talent. Not lack of ideas. Not lack of potential. Not even lack of time." (from a
  script)
- **Fitness coach:** "Not the diet. Not the program. Not even the scale. It was sleep."
- **Realtor:** "Not the price. Not the neighborhood. Not even the kitchen. It was the backyard."

### R12 · The Tab Count
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Shows the mental load as tabs left open. Everyone knows the feeling, so it lands in a second.
- **Fill in:** `Tabs open in my head: [three or four quick fragments].`
- **Meg's line:** "I'm working in stolen fifteen-minute windows during nap time while someone screams my name from
  the other room." (from a script)
- **Candle maker:** "Tabs open in my head: wick sizes, a wholesale email, the craft fair, dinner."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Open in my head right now: Bible study homework, a field trip form, and one
  unread text from my sister."

### R13 · Someone Like Me
- **Levers:** social proof, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Paints a very specific kind of person and asks how many there are. Viewers who match feel
  counted, and the crowd makes watching feel safe. Keep the traits odd and exact. The general version stalls.
- **Fill in:** `How many [people] are [one or two oddly specific traits] and quietly [the same thing]?`
- **Fitness coach:** "How many moms played college sports and now call carrying the laundry basket a workout?"
- **Realtor:** "How many of you have a saved house search you've never told your partner about?"

### R14 · The Small Number
- **Levers:** specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A small, exact number from the moment itself, never a win you're showing off. Odd, honest
  numbers read as true.
- **Fill in:** `[A small, exact number]. [What it holds]. [What it means].`
- **Meg's line:** "One tutoring client. One Etsy listing. One post. One freezer meal order. One coaching call. One
  person." (from a script)
- **Fitness coach:** "Eleven minutes. That's the whole workout. It's enough."
- **Candle maker:** "Two weeks. That's how long this candle cures before you get to smell it."

### R15 · The Tender Roast
- **Levers:** high-arousal emotion (funny), self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A warm, true joke on yourself lowers everyone's guard. Keep it kind and keep it true. Never
  invent a struggle you didn't have.
- **Fill in:** `Me: [the capable plan]. Also me: [the funny reality].`
- **Meg's line:** "When the baby makes the decision for you." (on-screen line, 240K+ plays, and the most shared per view of
  her short reels)
- **Realtor:** "Me: an expert at staging homes. My house: laundry on every chair."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Me: we're having a peaceful devotional this morning. The kids: a cereal war."

# Grow

Turns viewers into your people. Usually a talking reel, 45 to 100 seconds, that names a tension. Judge it by
shares, saves and follows.

### G1 · The Impossible Puzzle
- **Levers:** open loop, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Two true things that can't both be true, said plainly. A puzzle with no answer is a loop the
  viewer can't put down.
- **Fill in:** `I have a puzzle I can't solve. [True thing A]. [True thing B].`
- **Meg's line:** "I have an impossible puzzle to solve. I don't want to be a career mom. I don't want to be a
  stay-at-home mom." (spoken, 870K+ plays, 23K+ shares)
- **Fitness coach:** "I have a puzzle I can't solve. I want to train for a race. I don't want to miss bedtime."
- **Candle maker:** "Here's my puzzle. I want this to stay small and handmade. I also want it to pay the mortgage."

### G2 · The Third Door
- **Levers:** identity, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Turns down both options everyone offers, so the viewer feels the absence of the one that fits
  them.
- **Fill in:** `I don't want [option A]. I don't want [option B]. And nobody has a name for what's left.`
- **Meg's line:** "The thing I want is actually right here, in the middle, present and building at the same
  time." (spoken, 250K+ plays)
- **Realtor:** "I don't want to rent forever. I don't want a mortgage that runs my whole life. There has to be a
  middle."
- **Faith and family creator:** "I don't want a picture-perfect faith. I don't want to give up on it either."

### G3 · The Unsaid Sentence
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Flags a confession before it lands. Anything framed as a secret being handed over gets the
  viewer's full attention.
- **Fill in:** `I haven't said this out loud yet. [The quiet thing]. And I don't know what to do with it.`
- **Fitness coach:** "I haven't said this out loud yet. I don't miss the gym. I miss who I was there."
- **Candle maker:** "I haven't told anyone this. Some nights I pour candles just to have one quiet hour."

### G4 · The Quiet Pressure
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names a pressure the viewer carries every day and never says. They feel caught, in the good
  way.
- **Fill in:** `Nobody sees the pressure I put on myself to [prove or keep up with something]. It sounds like [the voice in my head].`
- **Realtor:** "Nobody sees the pressure to make every closing look easy. Some of them aren't."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Nobody sees the pressure to look like I have it together at church. Most
  Sundays I don't."

### G5 · The Exact Scene
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** The spoken version of the exact hour: the time, what the house is doing, and the quiet thing
  on your mind. See The Exact Hour for the on-screen version.
- **Fill in:** `It's [exact time]. [What the house is doing]. And I'm [the quiet calculation or feeling].`
- **Fitness coach:** "It's 4:58am. Everyone's asleep. And I'm deciding if this run is for me or for the scale."
- **Realtor:** "It's 10pm. The kids are asleep. And I'm refreshing a listing that isn't even for sale yet."

### G6 · The Small Tell
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A tiny habit gives away a big private pressure. The viewer catches themselves doing the same
  thing.
- **Fill in:** `The way I [small habit] gives it away. [What it gives away].`
- **Candle maker:** "I still call it 'just a hobby' when people ask. It paid for [something real] last month."
- **Faith and family creator:** "I still apologize when my kids get loud in church. Even when they're whispering."

### G7 · The Two Rooms
- **Levers:** open loop, specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Two versions of you in two rooms, and a door you can't hold open. Make the rooms real places,
  or the idea stays abstract.
- **Fill in:** `There's [self A] in [a real room]. There's [self B] in [another real room]. And I can't be in both.`
- **Meg's version:** "I cannot figure out how to exist in both of these realities at the exact same time." It
  stayed abstract, and it stalled at a few thousand plays. Name the rooms.
- **Fitness coach:** "There's the coach on the video call and the mom on the playroom floor. They never meet."
- **Realtor:** "There's the realtor taking showing calls and the mom in the pickup line. Same car. Same hour."

### G8 · The Unanswerable Question
- **Levers:** open loop · **Use it for:** opener or closer
- **What it does:** Ends on a real question you can't answer. It hands the loop to the viewer, and the comments do
  the rest.
- **Fill in:** `[Name the tension]. When does [the thing]? I really don't know.`
- **Meg's line:** "Solve the math for me. Because I really don't have the answer yet." (from a script)
- **Candle maker:** "When does a hobby become a business? The day someone pays? I still don't know."
- **Faith and family creator:** "How much screen time is too much on a sick day? I really don't know anymore."

### G9 · The Category Orphan
- **Levers:** identity, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener or the line people share
- **What it does:** Names a group that doesn't have a name yet. Viewers want to belong to the thing you just said
  exists.
- **Fill in:** `We're a new kind of [group]. We just don't have a name yet.`
- **Meg's line:** "We're a new category of moms. We just don't have a name yet." (spoken, the line people shared
  from her most-shared reel)
- **Fitness coach:** "We're a new kind of athlete. Forty minutes, a stroller and a playlist. We just don't have a
  name yet."
- **Realtor:** "We're a new kind of buyer. Two jobs, one kid, no free weekends. Nobody's named us yet."

### G10 · The Thing With No Name
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Describes a feeling so specific there's no word for it yet, and leaves it unfixed. Viewers
  stay for the word they've been missing.
- **Fill in:** `There's a feeling that hits [a specific window]. We don't have a word for it. It's [describe it, don't fix it].`
- **Meg's line:** "I want to be something that doesn't have a name yet." (from a script)
- **Candle maker:** "There's a feeling when you pack the last order of the night. It's not proud. It's not tired.
  It's both."
- **Faith and family creator:** "There's a feeling on the drive home from church when the kids fall asleep. I
  don't have a word for it."

### G11 · The Invisible Job
- **Levers:** self-relevance, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names the work nobody counts. The viewer finally feels seen for the job that never shows up
  anywhere.
- **Fill in:** `Nobody counts [the invisible work]. It doesn't show up [where work usually shows up]. It's the hardest thing I do all day.`
- **Fitness coach:** "Nobody counts the meal planning. It's not in the workout log. It's half the work."
- **Realtor:** "Nobody counts the 9pm calls talking a buyer through their nerves. They're not on any
  commission check."

### G12 · The Cost Nobody Sees
- **Levers:** loss, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** No regret about the choice, and an honest word about what it cost. Holding both at once is
  what makes people trust you.
- **Fill in:** `I don't regret [the choice]. Not for a second. And it cost me [the specific thing nobody sees]. Both are true.`
- **Meg's line:** "We sold our house. We're in a rental. We drive used cars. Not one regret. Period." (from a
  script)
- **Candle maker:** "I don't regret leaving my salary. Not once. It did cost me Friday lunches with my team."
- **Faith and family creator:** "I don't regret homeschooling. Not for a second. It did cost me the grown-up
  conversations I used to have every day."

### G13 · The Solidarity Check
- **Levers:** self-relevance, identity · **Use it for:** closer
- **What it does:** Asks, for real, whether they're the only one. It's the easiest comment in the world to leave,
  and it never feels like a pitch.
- **Fill in:** `[The private thing]. Tell me I'm not the only one.`
- **Meg's line:** "I just hope I'm not the only one who feels this way." (the closer on her most-shared reel)
- **Fitness coach:** "Tell me I'm not the only one doing lunges while the pasta boils."
- **Realtor:** "Please tell me I'm not the only one who drives past my first house on purpose."

### G14 · The Window Nobody Warns You About
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, naming the unnamed, specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Pins a feeling to one specific window of life that nobody warns you about. The window makes it
  concrete. The silence around it makes it a secret worth hearing.
- **Fill in:** `Why does nobody talk about the [unnamed thing] that hits during [a specific window]?`
- **Meg's line:** "Why does nobody talk about the career crash-out that hits in the middle of maternity leave?"
  (spoken, 170K+ plays, 3,000+ shares)
- **Fitness coach:** "Why does nobody talk about the dip that hits the week after your first race?"
- **Realtor:** "Why does nobody talk about the grief that hits the week you list your family home?"

### G15 · The Slow Confessional Burn
- **Levers:** open loop, identity · **Use it for:** the whole build
- **What it does:** A short label sits in the hook for the whole reel and names the category fast. That buys the
  spoken opener time to ease in slowly and stay unresolved. The label and the voice do different jobs.
- **Fill in:** On screen, the whole reel: the hook, with `[a short, repeatable category label]` as its small top
  line. Spoken, slow: `[a quiet, specific scene that eases into the tension]`.
- **Meg's line:** her Episode 1. A category label stayed up in her hook the entire reel while she eased in, and the
  name for the feeling arrived about a minute in. (870K+ plays)
- **Candle maker:** On screen: "the 9pm maker". Spoken: "The kids are down. The kitchen smells like cedar. And
  I'm wondering if this counts as a real job yet."
- **Faith and family creator:** On screen: "the tired believer". Spoken: "I used to pray in full sentences. Now
  it's mostly 'help' in the car line."

# Convert

Gets people to act. Usually teaching that pays off, then one ask. Judge it by comments per 1,000 plays on the
keyword.

### C1 · The Exact Order
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Promises the order, not just the list. Feeling overwhelmed is usually a sequencing problem, and
  the order is what people save.
- **Fill in:** `If I were [your person] who wanted [outcome] with only [a tiny constraint], here's the exact order I'd do it in.`
- **Meg's line:** "You don't need more time. You need to start in the right order." (her flip line)
- **Fitness coach:** "If I had twenty minutes a day to get strong again after a baby, here's the exact order."
- **Realtor:** "If I wanted to buy my first home in a year on one income, here's the order I'd do it in."

### C2 · It's the Order
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, loss · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Moves the blame off the person and onto the sequence. It lands as relief: nothing is wrong with
  you, the steps were just out of order. Never name "behind" or "lazy", not even to deny it.
- **Fill in:** `The reason [effort] isn't turning into [result] isn't [what they blame themselves for]. It's the order.`
- **Candle maker:** "If your candles aren't moving yet, it's probably not the scent. It's the order you launched
  them in."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Family devotions keep falling apart, and it isn't your kids. It's the order.
  Snacks first, then the Bible."

### C3 · The Real Problem
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Swaps the problem they think they have for the one they actually have, and the real one comes
  with a fix.
- **Fill in:** `You don't have a [problem they think they have]. You have a [real problem]. And that one has a fix.`
- **Meg's line:** "I don't think we have a balance problem. I think we have a design problem. And design problems?
  They have solutions." (from a script)
- **Fitness coach:** "You don't have a willpower problem. You have a 4pm snack problem. That one has a fix."
- **Realtor:** "You don't have a down payment problem. You have a timeline problem. That one has a fix."

### C4 · The Constraint Flip
- **Levers:** self-relevance, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Takes the thing they see as the reason they can't, and shows it's the reason this plan works.
- **Fill in:** `[The constraint] isn't why you can't. It's why this works.`
- **Meg's line:** "Mamas are not lacking vision and purpose. They're lacking time and energy." (her signature
  line)
- **Candle maker:** "A tiny kitchen isn't why you can't sell candles. It's why small batches work."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Ten minutes before the bus isn't too short for family prayer. It's the right
  length."

### C5 · The One Change
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** One specific cause behind a big result. One thing feels doable today. A hundred things never
  do.
- **Fill in:** `What changed [the result] wasn't [what they'd expect]. It was [one small, specific thing].`
- **Meg's line:** "But what I did change mattered." (from a script about posting nearly the same reel twice)
- **Fitness coach:** "What finally worked wasn't a new program. It was laying out my shoes the night before."
- **Realtor:** "What got our offer accepted wasn't the price. It was a one-line note about the garden."

### C6 · Skip This, Do That
- **Levers:** pattern interrupt · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A clear "skip this" breaks the scroll, and the swap right after it moves the reel forward, so it
  doesn't just startle and stall.
- **Fill in:** `Skip [the advice everyone gives]. Do [the specific swap] instead. Here's why.`
- **Fitness coach:** "Skip the hour-long workout. Do three ten-minute ones. Here's why."
- **Realtor:** "Skip the open houses this month. Drive the neighborhood at 7am instead."

### C7 · Not What You Think
- **Levers:** curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Tells them a familiar thing isn't what they think it is. They stay to find out what it
  actually is.
- **Fill in:** `Everyone thinks [a familiar thing] means [the assumption]. It doesn't.`
- **Candle maker:** "Everyone thinks a strong candle means more fragrance oil. It doesn't."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Everyone thinks quiet time has to be quiet. With toddlers, it doesn't."

### C8 · The Mistake I Made
- **Levers:** loss, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A loss frame pointed at yourself. "The mistake I made" teaches the same lesson as "the mistake
  you're making" without making anyone feel caught out.
- **Fill in:** `The mistake I made for [how long] when I tried to [outcome]: [the specific error].`
- **Fitness coach:** "The mistake I made for a year after my son was born: training like I was twenty-two."
- **Realtor:** "The mistake I made with my first house: falling for the kitchen and ignoring the roof."

### C9 · The Objection, Out Loud
- **Levers:** self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Says the viewer's objection before they can. Being read that closely lowers their guard before
  it goes up.
- **Fill in:** `You're thinking, "[their exact objection]." Here's why that's not the thing to worry about.`
- **Meg's line:** "You're looking at your list thinking, 'These all sound good. But which one do I actually start
  with?'" (from a script)
- **Candle maker:** "You're thinking, 'Nobody's paying thirty dollars for a candle.' Here's who is."
