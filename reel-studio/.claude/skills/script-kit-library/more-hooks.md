# More hooks (the bonus set)

Thirty-four more hook patterns, a bonus for Script Kit buyers: eleven for reach, ten for grow, thirteen for convert.
They work exactly like the Hook Library in `hook-library.md`: pick the job, check "Use it for", fill the brackets
with your own moment, words and facts, stack two or three levers (see `levers.md`), and run the pre-ship check in
`method.md` before you film.

There's no "Meg's line" here. The two examples on each pattern come from the Hook Library's working cast: a
fitness coach, a candle maker, a realtor, and a faith and family creator. They are examples, not real people, and
never lines to use, even in the same niche. Any number that would be a claim stays in brackets. The candle maker's lines show a pattern traveling into her own story. On a real product seller's account, a story like hers is told for her customers, and its hook block names them, never other makers (see "Who the reel is for" in `method.md`).

`hook sprint` reaches for these on a second sprint of the same idea, or when a job's fifteen in the Hook Library
don't fit.

# Reach

### R16 · The Tiny Revolt
- **Levers:** identity, high-arousal emotion · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** One small, quiet act treated like a line in the sand. The viewer feels the "good for her"
  lift, and that's the feeling people share.
- **Fill in:** `I did [one small, quiet thing]. It felt like [a full-on rebellion].`
- **Fitness coach:** "I left my watch at home on a run. It felt like breaking a rule I made up."
- **Candle maker:** "I stopped offering free shipping. It felt like staging a coup."

### R17 · The Mid-Thought Text
- **Levers:** self-relevance, pattern interrupt · **Use it for:** opener, often on screen
- **What it does:** Reads like a text they'd send their closest friend, with no setup and no polish. It passes the
  intimacy test in a second and skips the expert tone. Lowercase and a little messy is the point.
- **Fill in:** `[the exact text you'd send your best friend, mid-thought, no setup]`
- **Realtor:** "ok is it normal to fall for a house after one showing. asking for a client. and me."
- **Faith and family creator:** "is it weird that the church parking lot is where i do my best praying. genuinely
  asking."

### R18 · The Moving Finish Line
- **Levers:** loss, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names the "once this, then that" promise they keep pushing back. The viewer recognizes the
  finish line that keeps sliding in their own life.
- **Fill in:** `I keep telling myself, "Once [milestone], I'll [the thing I want]." [How far the milestone has slid.]`
- **Fitness coach:** "I keep telling myself, 'Once work calms down, I'll train for the half.' That was three work
  launches ago."
- **Candle maker:** "I keep telling myself, 'Once the holiday rush is over, I'll make a scent just for me.' The
  holiday rush ended in January. It's June."

### R19 · The Season Label
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, identity · **Use it for:** opener, or the line people share
- **What it does:** Gives a blurry in-between season a name of its own. The viewer gets a container for what
  they're living, and a phrase to repeat.
- **Fill in:** `Nobody ever named [this exact season]. So I'm naming it: [the name].`
- **Realtor:** "Nobody ever named the stretch between signing the offer and getting the keys. I'm calling it the
  hallway."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Nobody ever named the season when your faith goes quiet but doesn't leave. I'm
  calling it the whisper season."

### R20 · Right On Time
- **Levers:** identity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Takes the start they're living and reads it as right on time. It lands as permission. Give a
  truer read, not a pep talk, and never name late or behind, not even to deny it.
- **Fill in:** `You're right on time for [what they're starting]. [A kinder, truer read of where they are].`
- **Fitness coach:** "You're right on time for your first training block at 42. Your body finally has your
  attention."
- **Candle maker:** "You're right on time to open your shop at 50. You already know exactly what a good gift feels
  like."

### R21 · The Past-Me Glance
- **Levers:** open loop, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Looks back at the version of them who didn't know what was coming. The irony pulls the viewer
  in, because they're somewhere in that story too.
- **Fill in:** `The version of me who [believed or did the old thing] had no idea [what was about to change].`
- **Realtor:** "The version of me who swore I'd never sell my childhood home had no idea I'd be the one listing it."
- **Fitness coach:** "The version of me who hated running had no idea she'd cry at her first finish line."

### R22 · The Dated Decision
- **Levers:** open loop, specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A big decision with a date on it and nobody told yet. It's a live loop, and the viewer comes
  back to see which way it goes. It has to be real, and they have to share the ending later.
- **Fill in:** `[When] I'm [making the big decision], and I haven't told [anyone who'd have an opinion] yet.`
- **Candle maker:** "On Friday I'm closing my Etsy shop for good, and I haven't told a single customer yet."
- **Faith and family creator:** "This Sunday I'm stepping down from leading the women's group, and I haven't told
  our pastor yet."

### R23 · The Soft Brag
- **Levers:** social proof, specificity · **Use it for:** proof beat, right after the hook, or the caption
- **What it does:** A real win said plainly, with no round number and no fanfare. The restraint is what makes it
  believable. It never opens the reel: proof goes in the middle.
- **Fill in:** `[The win, said as plainly as possible]. [One line on what it meant].`
- **Fitness coach:** "My client ran the whole 5K without stopping. Her medal is on her fridge, and she sent me a
  photo of it."
- **Realtor:** "A family I helped last spring just sent their first holiday card from the new house. It's on my
  fridge."

### R24 · The Permission Slip
- **Levers:** identity, high-arousal emotion · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** They stop waiting for someone to say yes and give themselves the yes out loud. It's personal
  and quietly defiant, and the viewer wants the same slip.
- **Fill in:** `Nobody's going to give me permission to [want it / build it / call it real]. So I'm signing it myself.`
- **Candle maker:** "Nobody's going to hand me permission to charge what these candles are worth. So I'm signing it
  myself."
- **Fitness coach:** "Nobody's going to give me permission to rest on a Monday. So I'm signing it myself."

### R25 · The Quiet Start
- **Levers:** pattern interrupt, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A big move told with no fanfare at all. The understatement stands out in a feed full of
  announcements.
- **Fill in:** `No big post. No dramatic exit. I just [quietly started the thing].`
- **Realtor:** "No big post. No farewell party. I just started showing homes on Saturdays."
- **Faith and family creator:** "No big post. No announcement at church. I just started writing one prayer a day."

### R26 · The Daily Forecast
- **Levers:** high-arousal emotion (funny), self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener, often on screen
- **What it does:** Reports the inner weather as flatly as a forecast. It's funny, oddly precise and instantly
  relatable. Keep it true, and keep it kind to yourself.
- **Fill in:** `Today's forecast: [X]% [one state], [Y]% [the opposite state].`
- **Fitness coach:** "Today's forecast: 80% ready for leg day, 20% negotiating with the couch."
- **Candle maker:** "Today's forecast: 60% calm and lavender-scented, 40% dreading the post office line."

# Grow

### G16 · The Unexpected Grief
- **Levers:** loss, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names the grief of setting down a self they chose to leave. People share this loss and rarely
  hear it said out loud. On a grow reel, leave it unresolved.
- **Fill in:** `I chose to leave [the identity]. Nobody warned me I'd grieve it too.`
- **Realtor:** "I chose to leave the corporate job. Nobody warned me I'd miss having a desk with my name on it."
- **Fitness coach:** "I chose to stop competing. Nobody warned me I'd grieve the version of me with a race on the
  calendar."

### G17 · The Slow Fade
- **Levers:** loss, open loop · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A loss with no single dramatic moment, just a slow fade and one small tell. It stays with
  people because they can't pinpoint when it happened to them either.
- **Fill in:** `There wasn't one moment I [stopped / lost it]. It went [a little at a time], until [the small tell].`
- **Fitness coach:** "There wasn't one day I stopped moving. It went a class at a time, until the gym tag on my keys
  was the only proof."
- **Faith and family creator:** "There wasn't one Sunday I stopped singing. It went a verse at a time, until I
  noticed I was only mouthing the words."

### G18 · The Regret I'm Scared Of
- **Levers:** loss, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Swaps the obvious fear for the specific regret waiting down the road. A future loss hooks harder
  than any promise.
- **Fill in:** `I'm not afraid of [the obvious thing]. I'm afraid of [the specific regret down the road].`
- **Candle maker:** "I'm not afraid my shop will fail. I'm afraid it'll work and I'll never pour a candle just for
  me again."
- **Realtor:** "I'm not afraid of a slow market. I'm afraid of selling a hundred homes and never loving my own."

### G19 · The Stop-Cold Moment
- **Levers:** open loop, specificity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Years on autopilot, broken by one still moment. The viewer needs to hear what stopped them, so
  the payoff waits.
- **Fill in:** `For [a long time] I [ran on autopilot]. Then [one still moment]. And [what I saw] stopped me cold.`
- **Fitness coach:** "For five years I trained through every injury. Then a client asked why I never took my own
  advice. It stopped me cold."
- **Faith and family creator:** "For years I said yes to every church event. Then I sat in an empty sanctuary on a
  Tuesday and realized I hadn't prayed for myself in months."

### G20 · Wanting Both
- **Levers:** identity, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Asks whether it's okay to want two things at once. Wanting something you're not sure you're
  allowed to want is a feeling the viewer knows and has never said.
- **Fill in:** `Is it okay to want [one thing] and [the other thing]? Right now it feels like I have to apologize for one of them.`
- **Candle maker:** "Is it okay to love making candles for fun and want them to pay the mortgage? Right now it feels
  like I have to apologize for one of them."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Is it okay to love being home with my family and want a calling outside it? Right
  now it feels like I have to apologize for one of them."

### G21 · The After-Dark Dream
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** The want they only admit when nobody's around. Handing that to a stranger reads as trust.
- **Fill in:** `I only admit I want [the big thing] when [no one's around]. In daylight I call it [the smaller, safer version].`
- **Realtor:** "I only admit I want my own brokerage when I'm locking up after an open house. Out loud I call it
  maybe someday."
- **Fitness coach:** "I only admit I want my own studio when I'm the last one out of the gym. Out loud I call it a
  side thing."

### G22 · The Half-Sentence
- **Levers:** open loop, identity · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** A description of themselves that trails off halfway. The viewer finishes it in their own head
  and realizes they can't either.
- **Fill in:** `Someone asks what I do, and my answer trails off. "I'm a [role], and..." [nothing comes].`
- **Fitness coach:** "Someone at the pool asked what I do. I said, 'I'm a trainer, and...' Then I just smiled."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Someone at a dinner party asked what I do. 'I'm a pastor's wife, and...' That's
  where I always stop."

### G23 · The Swallowed Answer
- **Levers:** self-relevance, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** The honest answer they almost gave, and swallowed. It shows the self-editing the viewer does
  every day.
- **Fill in:** `Someone told me [the well-meaning thing]. I almost said [the honest thing]. I didn't. I never do.`
- **Candle maker:** "A friend said, 'It must be so relaxing, making candles all day.' I almost said I haven't sat
  down since Thursday. I didn't. I never do."
- **Realtor:** "A client said, 'You must love your flexible hours.' I almost told her I answer texts at midnight. I
  didn't. I never do."

### G24 · The Blurry Payoff
- **Levers:** open loop, loss · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** What they're giving up now for a future they can't picture yet. Some days it feels like faith,
  some days like a mistake, and they can't tell which. The worry stays open.
- **Fill in:** `I'm trading [something real right now] for a future I can't picture yet. Some days it feels like [faith]. Some days it feels like [a mistake].`
- **Fitness coach:** "I'm trading a steady gym paycheck for an online program nobody's bought yet. Some days it
  feels brave. Some days it feels reckless."
- **Candle maker:** "I'm trading my Saturdays for a shop that might never pay me back. Some days it feels like
  faith. Some days it feels like stubbornness."

### G25 · The Picture-Perfect Gap
- **Levers:** self-relevance, naming the unnamed · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** How it looks from the outside, how it feels on the inside, and both being true. It names a gap
  the viewer lives on social media every day. No fix at the end.
- **Fill in:** `From the outside it looks like [the dream]. Most days it is. And also [what the photo leaves out]. Both.`
- **Realtor:** "From the outside it looks like the dream. My own hours, my name on the sign. Most days it is. And
  also I ate lunch in the car between showings again. Both."
- **Faith and family creator:** "From the outside we look like the perfect church family. Most days we are. And also
  nobody saw the argument we had in the car on the way there. Both."

# Convert

### C16 · The Honest Odd List
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** An odd, exact count reads like the real number, not padding. The second half takes away the
  thing they dread.
- **Fill in:** `[An odd number] [things] that [get the result], and not one of them needs [the thing they dread].`
- **Fitness coach:** "Five moves that build real strength, and not one of them needs a gym."
- **Candle maker:** "Three ways to sell out a candle launch, and not one of them needs a big following."

### C17 · Ready Comes Second
- **Levers:** self-relevance, pattern interrupt · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Names the feeling they're waiting for, says it never comes first, and hands them a first step
  anyway. They feel seen, and the step feels doable. Never make them feel late for waiting.
- **Fill in:** `You're waiting to feel [ready / qualified / allowed] before you [start the thing]. That feeling doesn't come first. Start here.`
- **Realtor:** "You're waiting to feel qualified before you call a lender. That feeling comes after the call, not
  before. Start here."
- **Fitness coach:** "You're waiting to feel fit enough for a class. That feeling comes from the class. Start here."

### C18 · The Deciding Question
- **Levers:** curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Shrinks a big, overwhelming choice into one question. One question feels like a shortcut past
  months of going back and forth.
- **Fill in:** `Before you choose [the big decision], answer one question. It settles it.`
- **Realtor:** "Before you choose between two houses, answer one question: which kitchen do you see yourself in on a
  Tuesday night?"
- **Candle maker:** "Before you pick your next scent, answer one question: which one do your regulars ask you to bring
  back?"

### C19 · The Small Test First
- **Levers:** loss, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Takes the risk off their plate: a small, cheap test before the big build. Lower stakes make
  starting feel possible.
- **Fill in:** `Before you build [the big thing], try this [small, cheap] test. It'll tell you if it's worth it [soon].`
- **Fitness coach:** "Before you film a whole online program, run one free live class. It'll tell you if people will
  show up."
- **Candle maker:** "Before you order a hundred jars, pour ten and bring them to one market. You'll know by the end
  of the day."

### C20 · The Backward Map
- **Levers:** specificity, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Starts at the result they want and walks backward to the one step they can take today. The
  whole path shows at a glance, so it gets saved.
- **Fill in:** `Here's [the result they want], traced backward to the step you can take today.`
- **Realtor:** "Here's your first home, traced backward to the one document you can gather tonight."
- **Fitness coach:** "Here's your first pull-up, traced backward to the exercise you can do today."

### C21 · Wait, Do This First
- **Levers:** loss, pattern interrupt · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Catches them right before a move they're about to make, and hands them the step to do first.
  Urgency with real help in it, never a scare.
- **Fill in:** `If you're about to [the move they're about to make], pause and do [this] first.`
- **Candle maker:** "If you're about to buy wholesale wax, pause and test three wicks first."
- **Realtor:** "If you're about to make an offer tonight, pause and drive past the house after dark first."

### C22 · The Last Step First
- **Levers:** pattern interrupt, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Flips the usual order: the step everyone saves for last goes first. It contradicts what feels
  right, which stops the scroll and earns the why.
- **Fill in:** `[The step everyone saves for last], first. Here's why it changes the rest.`
- **Fitness coach:** "Pick your race date before you pick a plan. Here's why it changes the rest."
- **Candle maker:** "Take the product photos before you pour the batch. Here's why it changes the rest."

### C23 · The Small-Crowd Math
- **Levers:** specificity, self-relevance · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Breaks the belief that they have to go big first. The small, true numbers that actually work
  are a surprise and a relief. Every number has to be real.
- **Fill in:** `You don't need [a big number]. You need [a small number] of the right people. Here's the math.`
- **Fitness coach:** "You don't need ten thousand followers to fill a class. You need [number] of the right people.
  Here's the math."
- **Candle maker:** "You don't need a viral video to sell out a batch. You need [number] regulars. Here's the math."

### C24 · The One-Line Rule
- **Levers:** naming the unnamed, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener, or the line people share
- **What it does:** The whole lesson packed into one line they can repeat, then unpacked. The line is what travels.
  The unpacking keeps them watching.
- **Fill in:** `[One short rule, worth repeating]. Here's what it means for your next move.`
- **Realtor:** "Buy the street, not the kitchen. Here's what it means for your next showing."
- **Faith and family creator:** "Pray first, plan second. Here's what that looks like on a busy Monday."

### C25 · The Last One Standing
- **Levers:** self-relevance, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Turns "add more" into "cross off until one is left". Subtraction is a relief, and the one left
  standing is the lesson.
- **Fill in:** `Cross off every [option] except [the one that matters]. Here's how to tell which one stays.`
- **Candle maker:** "Cross off every scent on your list except the one people buy twice. Here's how to spot it."
- **Fitness coach:** "Cross off every workout in your plan except the one you'd do on your worst day. Here's how to
  find it."

### C26 · The Honest Timeline
- **Levers:** specificity, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** Pushes back on hype and on despair with a real, specific timeline. Believability stops the
  scroll. The timeline has to be true for them.
- **Fill in:** `Here's the real timeline from [starting point] to [first result]. Not the hype version.`
- **Realtor:** "Here's the real timeline from first showing to keys in hand. Not the TV version."
- **Fitness coach:** "Here's the real timeline from your first run to your first 5K. Not the app's version."

### C27 · I Tried It
- **Levers:** curiosity gap, social proof · **Use it for:** opener
- **What it does:** They take the risk so the viewer doesn't have to, and promise to show what happened. The test
  has to be real, and so does the result.
- **Fill in:** `I tried [X] so you don't have to. Here's what happened.`
- **Candle maker:** "I tried five kinds of wax so you don't have to. Here's what happened."
- **Fitness coach:** "I tried every free workout app for a month so you don't have to. Here's what happened."

### C28 · Watch This
- **Levers:** pattern interrupt, curiosity gap · **Use it for:** opener, spoken
- **What it does:** Promises a payoff they'll see happen on screen: the reel is its own demo, in one line. It's a
  spoken line. The thing happening is visible in the first frame, so the line points at it instead of warming up
  to it. The hook block on screen still names who it's for and the stakes, never "watch this".
- **Fill in:** `Watch what happens when I [do the specific thing they've wondered about].`
- **Realtor:** "Watch what happens to this living room when I move one couch."
- **Candle maker:** "Watch what happens when I pour the wax ten degrees cooler."
