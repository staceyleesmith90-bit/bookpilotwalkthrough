# The script scorecard

Twelve checks. `check my script` notes privately which ones hold and which could go further, each with one line
of why.

| # | Check | It passes when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The job | It does one job: reach, grow or convert. |
| 2 | Seen first | Something concrete lands first: a time, a place, an object, an exact number or a named moment, in the first sentence or in the first frame. A category of people on its own doesn't count, and no number about the creator goes in sentence one. |
| 3 | Three hook layers | Said, shown and read point at one question. The hook block names who it's for and never repeats the spoken line. |
| 4 | Levers | Two or three levers stack in the opening. |
| 5 | Register | Teaching pays off. Confessional stays open, with no lesson or hopeful turn bolted on. |
| 6 | Loops | A loop opens early and closes late, with a re-hook in the middle. |
| 7 | Pace | Something new lands every few seconds, and every line reads at a glance. |
| 8 | The demo | A teaching reel proves its point on screen, not only in words. A confessional passes with no demo, or one quiet visual at most: the words carry it. |
| 9 | The ask | One ask, after the payoff, with curiosity before any keyword. |
| 10 | Voice | It matches their Voice DNA, with no em dashes and no AI tells. Count every tell their Voice DNA limits (like "once per reel") and name any that go over. |
| 11 | Seen, not diagnosed | Nobody is made to feel behind, late, lazy or failing. |
| 12 | Length | It fits the job (see the three jobs in `method.md`). Shorter than usual passes when the loop has closed. |

## How to report it

The twelve checks are your working list. The person never sees them as a grade. They just wrote this script with
you, so it's solid work: the report shows them what's already strong and what could take it further. No score, no
count of checks, no pass or fail, and never the words weak, weakest, wrong, problem, fail or fix.

1. **What's already working:** two or three strengths, each quoting their line.
2. **Three optimizations to consider**, biggest lift first. Each one: the change, one line of why, and the new line
   written in their voice. Hook and job ideas come before polish. Fewer than three worth making? Say the rest is
   polish, or that it's ready to film as is.
3. **One line to try:** a stronger take on one line, offered as an option, with one line on what it adds.
4. One line: what to do next.

When the optimizations are made, refresh the Beats table and the Length line so the whole file matches the new
script.

When a check can't be judged from the page (like the demo on a teaching reel with no edit notes), offer it as an
optimization ("add an edit note for what's on screen here") rather than guessing it's there. Never suggest a demo for a confessional.
