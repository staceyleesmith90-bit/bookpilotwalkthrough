# The voice interview

For anyone short on transcripts: a creator just starting out, or one whose reels are mostly music and on-screen
text. Their answers become their words on the desk, so Claude can learn how they talk.

## How to run it

- One question per message. Ask them to answer the way they'd say it out loud to a friend, not the way they'd
  write it. Typing is fine. Dictation is better: on a Mac, press the dictation key or Fn twice; on a PC, press
  Windows key + H.
- Short answers are fine. A long, rambly answer is better. Their rambles are where their real phrases live.
- Swap "[topic]" for one of their topics from `script-desk/about-me.md` or their brand kit.
- "Skip" is always fine. Six answers is plenty. Stop early if they want to.
- They can also film themselves answering, drop the video in `script-desk/old-reels/`, and say
  `transcribe my old reels`. The engine transcribes it like any old reel.

## The questions

1. How did you get into [topic]? Tell it like you're telling a friend over coffee.
2. What do people get wrong about [topic]? Tell me like it bugs you a little.
3. Explain one thing about [topic] to someone brand new. Talk, don't write.
4. Tell me about a moment that made you think "this is why I do this."
5. Someone tells you they don't have the time or money for this. What do you say back?
6. What would you tell yourself from a couple of years ago?
7. Finish these however feels natural: "The truth is..." "Nobody tells you..." "I used to think..."
8. How do you cheer a friend on when they're about to do something scary?

## How to save it

Save each answer word for word under `## In my own words` in `script-desk/my-reels.md`, in the format in
`desk-files.md`. Never tidy, shorten or correct an answer. Their typos and fragments are the point.

Answers are also full of ideas. When an answer holds a story or a strong opinion, add one line for it to
`script-desk/ideas.md` (`from: interview`).
