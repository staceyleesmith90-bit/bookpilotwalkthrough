# Plan my week

First read `.claude/skills/script-kit-library/house-rules.md` and follow it the whole way through.

## 1. Read

`script-desk/about-me.md`, `script-desk/ideas.md`, `script-desk/my-reels.md` (their outliers and which jobs
worked), `script-desk/reels-i-like.md` (the shapes they love), `script-desk/voice-dna.md`, and from
`.claude/skills/script-kit-library/`: `method.md`, `levers.md`, `hook-library.md` and `edit-words.md`.

## 2. Ask the goal

If they didn't say it, ask one question: "What's the goal this week: more reach, more followers, or more
comments on [their keyword]?" Suggest one from their numbers so they can just say yes. No offer yet: ask only
reach or followers, and use the "Just starting, no offer yet" row whichever they pick. No convert reel until
there's something to offer.

If they name an offer or a keyword that isn't in `script-desk/about-me.md` yet, add it under "What I offer"
(start the file from `desk-files.md` if it's missing) and say so in one line.

## 3. Mix the jobs to fit the goal

| Goal | Reach | Grow | Convert |
|---|---|---|---|
| More reach | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| More followers | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| More comments on the keyword | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| Just starting, no offer yet | 3 | 2 | 0 |

For someone just starting, the goal is usually getting found and finding their people. Say so, and give each
reel an "Until then" ask from `about-me.md` instead of a keyword.

## 4. Pick the ideas

In this order:
1. Reels already scripted but not filmed yet (a script file with no Posted date).
2. Unscripted ideas in `script-desk/ideas.md`.
3. Their outliers from `my-reels.md`: keep the heart, change the shape. One strong idea can carry all three jobs
   in three shapes.
4. Shapes they love from `reels-i-like.md`, carrying their own topics.
5. Their topics from `about-me.md`.

Skip an idea that repeats the feeling of a reel they posted in the last two weeks. A new shape on the same topic
is fine.

## 5. Plan each reel

For each of the five: the job, the idea in one line, the hook (as they'd say it, concrete in the first sentence,
in their voice; on a b-roll reel, the hook block `Read: [who] / [line]`), the shape from `method.md`, the register
(a grow reel is confessional unless its story pays off), the demo in one line, and the one ask. Give one line of
why for each. No two reels in the week share a hook line or the same flip. A new idea you came up with
goes in as a moment or a topic, and its hook stays in "you" until they say the story is theirs.

## 6. Set the filming order

Batch by setup: talking reels together, b-roll together, same room, light and outfit together. Put the reel that
needs the most energy first. Say why in one line.

Keep every reel filmable where they already are, with what they have. An outfit or a prop they haven't mentioned
is a suggestion, never assumed (see the house rules). If they said when or how long they can
film, fit the plan to it. Otherwise put the reels that matter most for this week's goal first, so stopping early
still counts.

## 7. Save

- Before replacing an old `script-desk/week-plan.md`, copy anything filmed from it into that reel's script file
  (the "After posting" section) if it isn't there yet.
- Write `script-desk/week-plan.md` from `.claude/skills/script-kit-library/templates/week-plan-template.md`.
- Start a script file for each reel with the hook, demo and ask filled in, or update the file if one already
  exists, with its register on the top line. A b-roll reel's file says "Said: no voice" and "Length: about 5 to 8
  seconds", and its Script section holds the on-screen lines.
- Add new ideas to `script-desk/ideas.md` with their job tags, script paths and `from: plan my week (suggested)`.

## 8. Next

One line: "Next: `script this` on reel 1, or `hook sprint` any hook you want more options for."
