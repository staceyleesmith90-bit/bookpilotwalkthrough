# Hook shapes (Reel Studio's own set)

A hook is three things at once: the **first words** (said in under 2 seconds), the **first picture** (what the
viewer sees before a word lands) and the **cover line** (≤ 7 words on screen). Write all three; they should
agree, not repeat each other word for word.

| # | Shape | Use it when | Fill-in line | First picture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The tiny confession** | trust reels, personal stories | "I'll be honest: I ___." | close-up, a little too close |
| 2 | **The oddly specific number** | tips, results | "It took me ___ tries to ___." | the thing itself, mid-action |
| 3 | **Caught in the act** | funny / relatable life | "Watch what happens when ___." | the moment just before it happens |
| 4 | **The wrong way first** | teaching | "I used to ___ like this. Don't." | the wrong way, on screen |
| 5 | **The quiet flex** | proof, sell reels | "This is what ___ looks like now." | the result, no explanation |
| 6 | **Two truths** | contrast, before/after | "Expectation: ___. Real life: ___." | split second of the expectation |
| 7 | **Ask the viewer to check** | reach, broad problems | "Check your ___ right now." | their own version of the thing |
| 8 | **The insider word** | niche reach | "If you know what ___ means, this is for you." | a detail only insiders recognise |
| 9 | **Start at the end** | stories, transformations | "This is how it ended. Here's how it started." | the ending |
| 10 | **The small promise** | how-tos | "One ___ that saves you ___ every ___." | the saving happening |
| 11 | **Talking back to advice** | opinions, trust | "Everyone says ___. I tried it." | the advice written on screen |
| 12 | **The mid-sentence start** | storytime, calm reels | Start in the middle: "…and that's when she ___." | the reaction, not the cause |

## Rules of thumb
- The first word is never "So", "Hey guys", "In this video" or their name.
- Name a real thing: a time, a price, an object, a person ("my 8-month-old", "R49", "6:40 a.m.").
- A hook makes a promise; the last 3 seconds must pay it off. If it can't, change the hook.
- For faceless or music-only reels the hook is ALL picture + cover line: write the cover line and the first
  shot, skip the spoken words.
- Test two that are far apart (e.g. #3 and #9), not two versions of the same one.
