# Story bank

Follow the shared rules in `script-studio/SKILL.md`. The goal isn't a form filled in — it's a drawer full of
**real, specific things** to write from.

1. Read what exists (voice.md, brand, story-bank.md, recent scripts). Say back in 3 lines what you already know.
2. Ask, one at a time (tap answers + "type my own"), skipping anything already known:
   - **Who's watching?** "Who do you most want to stop scrolling?" → push for one real person: age-ish, their day,
     what's on their mind at 9 p.m.
   - **Your moments.** "Tell me 3 things that happened this week, even small ones." These become stories.
   - **Your things.** Objects, places, routines that keep showing up (the sandbox, the school run, the desk).
   - **Topics you could talk about for an hour** (3–5).
   - **What you sell or want them to do** — "nothing, just sharing" is a real answer.
   - **Proof:** results, numbers, kind messages, before/afters they have.
3. Write `my-scripts/story-bank.md`:
   ```
   # Story bank · updated <date>
   ## Who's watching      (one person, described like a friend)
   ## Moments             (dated one-liners; add new ones every session)
   ## Things & places     (recurring props — great show-me moments and AI elements)
   ## Topics
   ## Offer / ask         (or "no offer: growing + sharing")
   ## Proof
   ```
4. End with: "Your story bank is ready. Want 8 hooks for one of those moments?" → `hook-lab`.
Every later session: when they mention a new moment in passing, add it under Moments.
