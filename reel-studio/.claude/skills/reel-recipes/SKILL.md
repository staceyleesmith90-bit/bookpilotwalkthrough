# Reel recipes: idea → edit

Users describe ideas, not edits. Match the idea to a recipe, then write plan.json with the
treatments listed. Mix recipes when a reel has parts (e.g. story hook + tips body + CTA).

| The reel is… | Recipe (treatments) | Pacing | Captions | Sticker feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tips / list** ("3 ways to…") | `hook` · `badge:TIP 1/2/3` or `takeover:1.` per point · `sticker:auto` · `cta` | fast, punch-in every point | karaoke | badges + icons |
| **Story time** | `hook` (the tension) · plain lines · `note` for inner voice · `takeover` on the turning point · `reveal` before the payoff | medium, let emotion breathe | line | few, doodle |
| **Before → after / transformation** | `hook:Before…` · `card:Before` → `broll` of before · `reveal` · `card:After` → after · `sticker:sparkle` | slow → fast at the reveal | line | sparkle, arrow |
| **Myth vs fact / hot take** | `hook` · `badge:MYTH` + `takeover` · `sfx:hit` · `badge:FACT` · `emoji:no`/`check` | fast | karaoke | badges, emoji |
| **Tutorial / how-to** | `hook:How to …` · `label:Step 1` per step · `broll` of the action · `punch-in` on detail · `cta:Save this` | medium | line | arrows, circles, check |
| **Day in the life / vlog** | `title` (with-me / diary / episode) · `broll` every 1–2 lines · `title:location` · `label` time stamps ("7:02am") · `emoji` per moment · soft music | medium, cuts on beat | line or off | emoji, doodle |
| **Product / offer** | `hook` (the problem) · `takeover` on the result · `broll` product · `badge:$price` or `badge:NEW` · `cta:Link in bio` | fast | karaoke | badge, money, sparkle |
| **Motivational / quote** | faceless: `card` per line · slow fades · music, few SFX | slow | off | minimal |
| **Behind the scenes** | plain talking · `title:typed:POV…` · `note` for honesty · `emoji:eyes` | medium | line | playful |
| **Reaction / duet-style** | `broll` of the thing reacted to · `note` reactions · `sfx:boing` on punchlines | fast | single-word | emoji |

## Always
- **Open with a designed title** for vlogs, stories and lifestyle reels (`title-designer`);
  talking-head openers can put a big word **behind** the person. Tips/business reels open with
  a `hook` or a `typed` POV line.
- **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds**: `hook:` text that names the payoff or tension, not a
  greeting. If their first line is "Hi guys…", cut it or cover it with the hook.
- **One big moment per ~8–12 seconds**: takeover, card, reveal or b-roll — pattern interrupts
  keep people watching. Don't stack two big moments back to back.
- **Stickers match meaning** (money line → coin, time → clock). Auto handles the obvious ones;
  you add emotional ones (heart, eyes, sparkle) and draw missing ones.
- **Sound follows action**: every pop-in gets a soft sound, transitions get whooshes, the
  reveal gets riser → hit, CTA gets a ding. The planner does this; keep ≤ ~1 SFX per 2s.
- **End with a CTA** that matches the reel's goal (follow / comment keyword / save / link).
- Match **inspiration** if they saved one: its pacing (cuts/min), grade and treatments win
  over these defaults.

## Explaining the plan
Describe it in their words: "Big bold text on your money line, a little coin doodle, a quick
zoom when you say 'here's the thing', and a ding at the end with 'Comment GUIDE'."
