# Write my script

Shared rules: `script-studio/SKILL.md`. Structures: `script-studio/reel-shapes.md`.

1. Inputs: the idea/hook (or their ramble — keep their best phrases word for word), the job, the length
   (default 25–35 s), talking or faceless. Ask only what's missing, one tap question at a time.
2. Pick the reel shape that fits the job; say which in one line ("This is a *little story*: …").
3. Write the **script card** into `my-scripts/<date>-<slug>.md`:
   ```
   # <working title> · job: reach|trust|sell · ~30 s · shape: <shape>
   Cover: "<≤7 words>"
   | # | Say (word for word) | On screen | ~s |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | <hook line> | <first picture> + text "<cover>" | 2 |
   | 2 | … | … ★ show-me moment | … |
   Ask: <one line, matches the job>
   ## Film it
   - shots to grab (incl. 3–5 b-roll shots of their things/places), framing (head in the top third, text room),
     light, one take or line by line, what to hold up to the camera
   ## Studio plan (the editor reads this)
   - beats → effect per beat, picked from what fits: freeze + arrow, object effects (receipt_print,
     label_tape, sticky_notes, stamp_card) for any list, paper collage for titles, b-roll cutaways
   - AI elements: 4–8 things from the story to draw as one sticker set (style: <one>)
   - cover: kicker + title, which shot
   - music / sound feel in 3 words
   ```
4. Count the words (2.5 words/s). Over → cut a beat, not the hook or the ask.
5. Read it back as *one* short paragraph of what the viewer will feel, then ask: *Love it · Change a line ·
   Shorter · Check it* (→ `script-check`). After filming: "drop the clips in or paste the Drive link and say
   *edit this*."
