# Will this use a lot of Claude credits?

Short answer: **it's designed to use as little as possible.** The heavy work happens on your
own computer, which is free and unlimited.

| Runs on your computer (free) | Uses your Claude plan |
|---|---|
| Transcribing your voice | Chatting with Claude about the reel |
| Cutting pauses, "um"s and retakes | Claude writing the style plan (a few lines) |
| Captions, stickers, layout, sound effects | Claude looking at an image when it needs to judge style |
| Rendering the final video and cover | Studying inspiration reels (one image per reel) |
| The review page and the drag-and-drop editor | Onboarding interview (once) |

## What makes a reel "light"
- Film, drop it in, approve the plan, small tweaks → a short conversation.
- Using the drag-and-drop editor for fine adjustments (no Claude usage at all).

## What makes a reel "heavy"
- Many rounds of "actually, redo everything in a different style".
- Detailed frame-by-frame studies of long reference videos.
- Very long videos (10+ minutes of footage) with lots of back-and-forth.

## If you hit your plan's limit
Your projects are saved. You can still open the editor, adjust and render. When your limit
resets, carry on where you left off. You can also move up a plan any time and move back down.

We can't promise an exact number of reels per month: it depends on your plan and how much
you change each reel. Most people get plenty on the standard plan when they approve the
style plan and use the editor for small tweaks.
