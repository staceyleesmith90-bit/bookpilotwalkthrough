Reelmate

Your reel-editing buddy. You film it, Reelmate edits it, with Claude.

What you need

A Mac or Windows computerThe Claude desktop app (Pro plan or higher)Your Reelmate key (from your welcome page)

Set up once (about 5 minutes)

1

Download

On your welcome page, tap Download Reelmate for Claude.

2

Double-click it

Claude opens and asks to install. Tap Install, then paste your key.

3

Say hi

In Claude, say "hi Reelmate". It sets itself up and asks a few easy questions about your look.

Every reel

1

Add your clips

Drop them in, or paste a Google Drive link for big videos from your phone.

2

Say what it is

One line or a voice note: "Liara's first day in the sandpit".

3

Tick the storyboard

See every moment before it's built. Keep what you love, add a note.

4

Get your reel

Captions, stickers, sound and a cover, ready to post or save.

Made for

Creators

Talking to camera, b-roll, tips and stories in your brand.

Brands

One workspace per client: products, services and offers, even when no one talks.

Memories

Your day's photos and clips become a keepsake video for your camera roll.
