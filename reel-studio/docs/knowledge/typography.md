# Typography for reels

## Three fonts, three jobs
- **Main** — hooks, takeovers, cards. The scroll-stopper: characterful, heavy, readable huge.
- **Accent** — thought bubbles, labels, the "wink". Script, handwritten or italic.
- **Caption** — read-along captions. Plain, heavy enough to read over video in half a second.

## Pairing rules
- The two fonts must be **clearly different** — serif + sans, or heavy sans + handwritten.
  Two similar sans-serifs is the classic amateur mistake.
- **Only one loud font.** If Main is loud, Accent is small and light, Caption is neutral.
- Hierarchy through **size and weight**, not more fonts. Max 3 fonts per reel (we use 2–3).
- **Case is a style choice**: UPPERCASE = energy (bold, playful), lowercase = calm/editorial,
  Sentence case = neutral/minimal. Set per pack (`"case"`).
- **The half-second test**: can you read the takeover in half a second on a phone at arm's
  length? If not, fewer words or a heavier font.

## On-video legibility
- Text over footage gets an outline/shadow automatically (`on_video`).
- Keep text inside the safe zone (top 220px and bottom 440px are covered by app UI).
- 3–7 words per takeover; captions 1–4 words per line.
- Highlight **one** keyword (pop colour), not every word.

## Licensing
Only use fonts that are free for commercial use (OFL/Apache — Google Fonts) or that the user
owns a licence for. Never extract fonts from CapCut, Canva or other apps.

## Captions that pop (studied from CapCut caption tutorials)
- Two fonts: a clean primary (context) + a secondary script/serif for the EMOTION word, title case,
  ~1.4× bigger, brand colour. "If every word is treated the same, nothing stands out."
- Primary: uppercase, slightly tight tracking, light outline + ~70% shadow; not the heaviest weight
  (SemiBold/Bold reads premium; ExtraBold everywhere feels shouty).
- Motion: words appear as spoken (pop-in with a small overshoot, or blur/slide in ~0.2–0.6 s);
  keep 2–3 reusable movements and repeat them — consistency looks designed.
- Highlight 1 word per line: colour, box, or script — different brand colours for different
  keywords keep long videos lively. Emojis optional, sparingly.
Reel Studio styles: pop · duo · rainbow · boxed · popin · stack (+ karaoke, highlight, bounce,
elegant…). `"weight"` in captions picks Medium/SemiBold/Bold/ExtraBold.
