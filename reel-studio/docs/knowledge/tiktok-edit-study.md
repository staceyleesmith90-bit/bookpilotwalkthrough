# Study: 13 CapCut edits on TikTok (chosen by the product owner as "this is the level")

Analysed frame-by-frame (16 frames each), cut rate, sound-event rate and speech. Principles
only — no assets copied.

## Text is designed, not subtitled
- Each caption line is a small **layout**: the key word HUGE (tall condensed display, script or
  italic serif, brand/accent colour), the other words small caps around it, stacked on 2–3 lines
  of different sizes. (kayzee.edits, the_creative.gina, imdayisbz)
- **Mixed fonts inside one phrase**: bold sans + italic serif + script ("Edit *like a* PRO",
  "*no necesito* media casa").
- **Text moves around the screen**: each line lands in a different zone (upper-left, right,
  centre, lower-left), sometimes running slightly off the edge; never one fixed caption bar.
- Talking-head tutorials keep captions **minimal** (1–2 words, small, centre) when the screen is
  busy with other elements (floofysocials, dun.kay pink pill captions).
- Titles: tracked serif caps + a script word over it ("EDITING *Tutorial*", "TUTORIAL *Fashion
  Reel*", "STORY *in my feb*") — elegant, small, never shouty.

## Layout and elements
- The speaker is often **framed as a card** (rounded, glow or grid background, phone mockup) so
  text and b-roll cards have room: before/after side by side (ANTES/DESPUÉS neon frames),
  desk-mockup frames, "Edited on CapCut" phone frames.
- **Pop-up cards** (screenshots, product shots on white cards, comment bubbles) appear in
  different corners around the speaker, then leave. A comment-collage makes a strong hook.
- Brand stories = motion design: brand colours as full-screen fields, cut-out B/W photos with
  name tags, single giant words ("FAILURE.", "EVERYWHERE"), a boxed year, logo reveal at the end.

## Pace and sound
- Montage/photo-dump templates cut on the beat (~2 cuts/s); talking videos keep one take and
  change the picture with elements instead.
- Edit-heavy videos carry 2–4 sound events per second (pops, clicks, swipes, whooshes tied to
  each element); a specific transition is paired with a specific sound.

## Built into Reel Studio from this study
- `"captions": "kinetic"` — designed layouts (stack / script / italic / side) that rotate and
  land in different zones, clear of faces; words appear as spoken; key word ticks.
- Framed-card layout (speaker on a rounded card over a blurred copy) and pop-up product cards
  in the corners, chosen automatically from product moments.
- Comment-bubble hook (`comment:` / `comments:`), from real comments only.
- Brand-story template (`brand-story`): giant words, boxed year, founder card, logo reveal.
- Intro (fade in, gentle pull-out), fade-out ending and a brand end screen on every reel.
- Next: beat-cut photo dumps with title pairs.

## The owner's favourite style (diaryoflov3, achama__, + 1 template) — "aesthetic vlog"
- Music-led, little or no talking; 30-130 cuts a minute (0.45-0.8 s clips of each step/product).
- **No word-by-word captions.** One quiet label held the whole video: small bold sans title
  ("GRWM for School") + a coloured typewriter date line — centred, like a watermark.
- Openers: chunky rounded title + script line ("A GLIMPSE *of today*"), torn-paper edges, or a
  colour-block wipe into a letter-by-letter "VLOG".
- Sound: small real-world sounds on actions (click, typing, camera shutter, sparkle, paper tear,
  keypad) — the creator's own CapCut searches show exactly this list; very few whooshes.
- Built: title template `tag` (held label), inspiration profiles with little speech now switch
  to vlog mode (captions off, held label, clip length from the reference, playful sound kit).
