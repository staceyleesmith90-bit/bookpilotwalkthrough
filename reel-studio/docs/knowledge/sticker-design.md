# Sticker design for reels

What makes app sticker libraries (CapCut, Instagram, TikTok) feel polished is not the
individual drawings — it's **consistency, pairing and timing**. Study those, then draw our own.

## 1. What a sticker is for
A sticker should do one of these jobs. If it does none, leave it out.
- **Say it again visually** — the word "coffee" + a cup. Doubles comprehension for silent viewers.
- **Point** — arrow, circle, underline at the thing that matters.
- **Feel** — heart, sparkle, eyes, sweat drop: the emotion under the words.
- **Label** — badge with a number, price, step, "NEW", "MYTH/FACT".
- **Proof** — cut-out photo of the result, screenshot, product.

## 2. Style families (choose one per reel; the pack decides)
| Family | Looks like | Why it works | Our style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand-drawn doodle | marker lines, slight wobble | human, warm, "made by a person" | `doodle` |
| Flat colour | solid fills + dark outline | reads instantly at small size | `flat` |
| Retro / 70s–80s | flat + hard offset shadow, warm palettes, bubbly shapes | nostalgic, playful, very current | `retro` |
| Neon / glow | glowing thin lines on dark | nightlife, luxe, beauty | `neon` |
| Emoji | platform-style glossy icons | casual, universal, reaction-y | `emoji` |
| Badges & labels | text in bursts, pills, tags | structure: lists, prices, steps | `badge:` |
| Die-cut | thick white outline around anything | "real sticker" look, separates from busy video | pack `die_cut` |
| Photo cut-out | real object/person with outline + arrow + scribbled label | proof and personality | overlay |

Current trends (2025–26 sticker/print market): warm-retro type and shapes, hand-drawn
imperfect illustration, earth tones (terracotta, sage, mustard), and on the other side clean
minimal brand-first stickers. Both work — the brand decides. (Sources in `sources.md`.)

## 3. Pairing rules (the part apps get right)
- **One family per reel.** Mixing doodle + 3D emoji + neon looks random. Exception: badges pair
  with any family because they're typographic.
- **Stickers use the brand palette**, max 2 colours per reel (pop + accent), alternating.
- **Line weight matches the fonts**: heavy fonts → thick strokes; thin serif → thin strokes.
- **Size hierarchy**: text is the hero; stickers are 20–30% of the frame width, badges a bit
  bigger when they carry the number.
- **Placement**: near the text they relate to (top-right corner of a takeover is classic),
  never on the face, never in the app UI zones, slightly rotated (−8° to +9°) so they feel
  placed by hand.
- **Rhythm**: stickers arrive on the word, with a pop + soft sound, and leave with the line.
  Idle stickers get a gentle float so the frame never feels frozen.
- **Restraint**: one sticker per line at most; skip lines that are already busy.

## 4. Drawing good stickers (for new SVGs)
- Start from the **silhouette**: would you recognise it as a black blob at 100px? If not,
  simplify.
- 2–6 strokes. Closed shapes filled with `{paper}` (or the pack colour in flat/retro).
- One detail in `{pop}` (steam on coffee, shine on a coin, flame tip).
- Slight asymmetry and uneven curves = hand-made. Perfect circles look like clip-art.
- Consistent corner radius / round caps across the set.
- Test on light and dark backgrounds (`brand-preview` with two packs).

## 5. Concept → sticker cheat sheet
time/hours → clock · money/price/paid → coin, `badge:$…` · idea/tip → lightbulb, `badge:TIP` ·
coffee/morning → cup · growth/results → arrow up, chart emoji · love/family → heart ·
wow/new → sparkle, star · done/easy → check · warning/mistake → `emoji:warning`, `badge:MYTH` ·
phone/social → phone · film/content → camera · work/online → laptop · question → thought bubble ·
secret → `emoji:eyes` · fast → rocket (draw it!) · home → house (draw it!) · baby/kids → `emoji:baby`.
