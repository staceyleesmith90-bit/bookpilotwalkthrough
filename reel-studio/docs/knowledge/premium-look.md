# The premium look — what separates "wow" from "cheap"

Studied from high-end reels (beauty/luxury brands, Apple-style product films, "aesthetic"
CapCut templates, top creator edits). Use these rules on every reel unless the brand is
deliberately playful.

## Graphics
- **Less, bigger, calmer.** 1 graphic per idea, max 2 on screen, ~1 every 4–6 s. Empty space is premium.
- **No thick white die-cut borders, no clip-art, no rainbow.** Those read as kids' stickers.
- **Frosted glass** (blurred video behind, dark 30% tint, hairline light edge, deep soft shadow)
  for labels, prices, claims. One brand-colour dot is the only colour.
- **Callouts** for products: dot on the product → hairline → tracked small caps label
  (+ light sub-line). Feels like an ad, not a sticker.
- **Type does the decorating:** one italic serif word ("so comfy"), tracked caps ("ULTRA THIN"),
  or a serif + script title. Two font families per reel, max.
- **Colour:** brand colour as an accent only (dot, keyword, underline) — never big fills.
- **Motion:** fade/slide 0.25–0.4 s with ease-out; no bouncing on premium brands.

## Picture
- Correct first (white balance, exposure, contrast), then grade. A colour cast screams "phone video".
- Subtle finish on everything: fine sharpening, a whisper of grain, soft vignette.
- Steady footage (stabilise handheld), face in the upper third, punch-ins between 1.06–1.26.
- Every 2–4 s something changes (cut, zoom, graphic, keyword) — never 5 s of nothing.

## Sound
- Voice first: denoise to the room's measured noise, remove hum, gentle compression, −14 LUFS.
- Music under the voice, audible in pauses; SFX soft (a click/whoosh, not cartoon pops).

## Captions
- Short lines (≤ 17 characters), bold, outlined, 1 keyword per line in the brand colour.

## In Reel Studio
Default `look: premium` → sticker_style `glass`, badges `glass`, custom words → serif word,
treatments `callout:LABEL|sub@x,y`. Playful brands: `"look": "playful"` (brand's own sticker style).
